## Why

The demo real-button pilot is useful only if its result is explicit: accepted
evidence, candidate evidence, a rejected unsafe target or a V3 mutation blocker.
Publication prevents a blocked or candidate pilot from being mistaken for
accepted protocol knowledge.

## What Changes

- Publish the final demo-button pilot decision with target, classification,
  capture or blocked outcome, recovery status and residual risk.
- Update protocol docs or evidence indexes with compact reviewed links.
- Keep accepted mapping output empty unless replay, direct Python-manager probe
  or typed contract evidence supports the same non-mutating action.
- Route business mutation or rollback-dependent results to V3 or later
  mutation/recovery work.
- Keep raw runtime output and generated replay payloads outside reviewed git.
