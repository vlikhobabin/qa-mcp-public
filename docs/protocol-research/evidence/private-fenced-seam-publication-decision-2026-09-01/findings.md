# Private fenced-seam publication decision

## Decision

**Outcome A: authorize exactly one later, separately accepted private
fenced-seam extraction/composition relative to clean published HEAD.**

The authorization is conditional and design-only. The later seam card must
start from published clean HEAD, own only the exact paths and source predicates
below, compile and pass the hostile offline floor as a standalone reviewed
tree, and publish before I4 is replanned against it. I5 implements no seam,
classifier, diagnostic, runtime correction or test payload.

Clean HEAD already has a real worker consumer with exactly two direct inventory
call sites. The candidate's pure fenced sample and Windows wrapper are
syntactically independent of transferred-observer, diagnostic-checkpoint and
marker-derivation predicates. That establishes one bounded composition
candidate: publish the private fence, its exact liveness/diagnostic ownership
closure and substitutions at those two existing call sites from clean HEAD,
while excluding every unrelated dirty-candidate byte.

## Evidence boundary

The decision used only:

- published I3 card, archive and synced decision specification at commit
  `8f2bce4f4f49b6c80b565bec0ce15b61a38e14fa`;
- the blocked I4 card and active artifacts;
- retained privacy-safe I4 composition evidence under ignored
  `.runtime/changerail/` state; and
- bounded `git show`, `git grep`, `git diff`, `rg` and hash analysis of clean
  HEAD and the unchanged local candidate.

No 1C process, endpoint, target, real Windows configuration, live retry,
confirmation or S7 surface was used. No protocol capture, frame range, replay
or new runtime observation exists for this decision. The retained target
identity remained inaccessible evidence lineage only.

The ignored I5 evidence index records the clean/dirty predicates, current
remote readiness, manifest ownership, supplied baselines and the review-cycle-1
protected snapshot. That runtime index is excluded from publication.

## Exact clean-HEAD boundary

Clean HEAD provides these admitted dependencies:

- `hiddenWorkerLifecycleResponse`;
- `hiddenWindowIdentity` and `hiddenWindowIsolationReceipt`;
- `inventoryHiddenWindowIsolation`;
- existing main-window admission functions; and
- `observeHiddenDirectInWorker`, with exactly two direct
  `inventoryHiddenWindowIsolation` calls.

Clean HEAD lacks the four seam families
`hiddenDirectFencedSample`, `observeHiddenDirectFencedSample`,
`observeHiddenDirectWindowsFencedSample` and
`hiddenDirectPreReceiptDiagnostic`. The candidate's pure sample and Windows
wrapper do not reference `runHiddenWorkerTransferredObserver`,
`hiddenWorkerAfterControllerTransfer`, `deriveHiddenDirectMarkerHash`, a
checkpoint reporter or the diagnostic schema. Therefore the two existing
clean-HEAD inventory consumer sites can be composed with the bounded seam by a
later clean-base card; the dirty worker's larger combined hunk is not the unit
of extraction.

No prospective seam was implemented or compiled in I5. Compilation and the
connected hostile oracle are mandatory admission gates of the later seam card,
not facts claimed by this decision.

## Dependency ceiling

The future seam tree is capped at:

- clean published I5 HEAD plus the seven owned paths above;
- at most three changed non-test Go files and four changed test Go files;
- no new package, module, external dependency, public caller, route or wire
  field;
- at most 250 added non-test Go lines across the exact predicates;
- the existing Go standard library and already-published `x/sys/windows`
  dependency only; and
- no marker derivation, action, retry, fallback, desktop/thread/window-station
  mutation, process/job ownership expansion or live target behavior.

Crossing any ceiling invalidates this authorization and requires a new
architectural investigation rather than a larger seam patch.

## Hostile verification floor

The future seam card must retain one clean-HEAD RED and the same-tree GREEN
oracle, all offline:

1. RED proves clean HEAD lacks the four seam families and the worker still has
   two direct inventory calls.
2. GREEN compiles the exact seven-path prospective tree and proves the worker
   has exactly two fenced-wrapper calls and no direct inventory bypass inside
   `observeHiddenDirectInWorker`.
3. Injected rows cover child/listener live, exited and unknown; inventory
   error, empty and non-empty; post-fence change; main absent/exact; handle
   close failure; unknown/malformed diagnostic state; and both first/second
   consumer boundaries.
4. Instrumentation proves one inventory call per admitted boundary, exact
   pre/post fence counts, zero retry/fallback/action, unchanged fail-closed and
   passive-UIA outcomes, and exact transferred-observer job/TPort ownership.
5. Privacy hostiles prove no raw error, endpoint, desktop, handle, PID, port,
   UI value or other dynamic identity enters the diagnostic.
6. Focused/full host-agent Go test and vet, Linux tests, deterministic Windows
   amd64/386 test cross-builds, strict OpenSpec, manifest scope, staged-tree
   compile and diff/whitespace checks all pass without launching Windows or 1C.

The oracle must execute the actual two worker call sites. A pure-function-only
test, source-text-only assertion or dormant callback is insufficient.

## Fail-closed staging and later I4 rule

The seam must be delivered from a clean published-HEAD context after I5. It
must not be staged from this dirty workspace and must never stage a whole
dirty candidate file. Before review, its explicit staging plan must contain
only the seven allowed paths, and a hunk audit must map every added/changed
region to one named source predicate above. The exact staged tree—not the
ambient dirty tree—must compile and pass the hostile floor.

Any extra path, unowned hunk, dependency-ceiling breach, direct-inventory
bypass, missing diagnostic connection, compile failure or hostile failure is a
safety stop. It does not authorize importing predecessor bytes or weakening
the oracle.

Only after the seam card is independently reviewed and published may I4 be
replanned against that published seam commit. The current dirty I4 payload and
artifacts remain excluded; they may not be staged or treated as already
composed authority.

## Rollback

Rollback of the future seam is the exact reverse of its seven-path staged
patch: remove only the inserted private types/helpers/tests/callback and
restore the two worker call sites to the clean-HEAD direct inventory form.
Rollback must reproduce the pre-seam clean HEAD tree for all seven paths and
must not touch any protected dirty payload.

Rollback of I5 removes only its card, curated findings, archived OpenSpec
change and synced decision spec. I5 has no source or runtime rollback.
